🍕 free.food-bot

We run free.food, a site that helps students find campus events that offer free food. This is the bot that reads the public event calendars it lists.

What it reads

  • Public event calendars only: {{FEEDS}} feeds at {{CAMPUSES}} universities, as iCal, RSS or the calendar's own public JSON. No logins, no private pages, no personal data.
  • Each feed at most once every {{HOURS}} hours, a few pages at a time, with long pauses on failure.
  • Every event we show links back to its page on your site, and names the calendar it came from.

How to recognise it

Every request it makes carries this User-Agent:

{{UA}}

robots.txt

We read public event feeds, the calendars universities publish for people to find their events, and for now we read them even where a site's robots.txt rule for all bots discourages it. A rule written for us by name is always obeyed:

User-agent: free.food-bot
Disallow: /

Add that, and we stop reading your site at our next check.
